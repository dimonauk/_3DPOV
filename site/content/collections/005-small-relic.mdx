Between the fieldwork and the gallery sits the workshop. **Small Relic,
Hand** is the object that lives there: a palm-scale cast taken directly
from one of the studio rig maquettes, finished in cold-cast bronze with
a waxed patina, set on a short oak plinth.

It is the Protocol's desk companion. It is not editioned to scarcity but
to presence &mdash; thirty casts, each numbered and signed on the
underside of the plinth. If the plates are the Protocol made public,
this is the Protocol made personal.
