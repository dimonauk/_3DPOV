Low water at Bankside is a room. The foreshore becomes ground; the
embankment wall becomes cornice; the river, absent, becomes ceiling. The
kata here is the simplest of the Protocol &mdash; **Standing Figure** &mdash;
and the hardest. One body, still, held against the water for the full
length of one tide.

The rig rotates in place. For six hours and twenty-eight minutes the
lamps trace a slow cylinder into the dark. The figure does not move. The
city does: a pair of foxes along the wall at 01:40, a river police boat
at 03:11, a sudden hard rain for eleven minutes beginning at 04:26.

What the plate holds is not the figure. It is the volume of air the
figure displaced. **Iron Gospel** is the name the foreshore gave it
back.
