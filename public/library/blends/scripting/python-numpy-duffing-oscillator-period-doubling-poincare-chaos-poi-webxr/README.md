# Duffing Oscillator — Period-Doubling Route to Chaos, Poincaré Sections & Basins as Poi Light-Trail (Blender 5.1)

The double-well Duffing oscillator is one of the canonical systems for studying chaos in continuous time.  
Its equation `ẍ + 0.30ẋ - x + x³ = γcos(1.2t)` describes a damped particle in a Mexican-hat potential driven by a sinusoidal force.  As the driving amplitude γ increases, the system goes through a period-doubling cascade — a sequence of bifurcations at γ₁, γ₂, γ₃ … converging geometrically at the Feigenbaum ratio δ ≈ 4.669 — before entering broadband chaos.

## Key maths

- Potential: V(x) = −x²/2 + x⁴/4 — two stable wells at x = ±1, saddle at x = 0.
- Integration: 4th-order Runge-Kutta, 400 sub-steps per forcing period — preserves the fractal basin structure that first-order Euler would smear.
- Feigenbaum constant: γ_{n+1} − γ_n → (γ_n − γ_{n−1}) / δ, δ ≈ 4.669.
- Poincaré section: stroboscopic sampling at t = nT gives an area-contracting map; the attractor's fractal structure has box-counting dimension ≈ 1.3.
