A wall array is a single kata broken across many hands. Each panel in
**Choir of Ten Thousand Lights** carries one fragment of a long gesture
&mdash; a second of the body's turn, a second of the lamp's passage. Hung
in sequence, the panels reassemble the kata as an architecture.

Arrays are sold in configurations of three, five, seven, or nine panels.
Each configuration ships with a unified low-voltage bus, mounting
hardware templated to the wall, and a printed sequencing guide. Panels
are matched at dispatch: a three-panel set is a three-panel kata, not
three pieces of a nine.

Larger configurations on commission.
