The night watch is the oldest kata in the Protocol. A single figure, a
single lamp, a single door held through the darkest twelve hours of the
year. The lamp does not move. The lamp does not sleep. The city passes.

**Lamp of the Watch** is the object that remembers. A cast body, palm-
sized, seeded with acrylic waveguides that carry a warm ember from a
concealed ring of LEDs. A firmware routine released with the object
cycles the ember across the twelve hours between dusk and dawn, slowest
at the hinge of the night. Plug it in at 20:00. It will cross a solstice
with you.

This is the first object in the Protocol. It is not a print. It is a
watch.
