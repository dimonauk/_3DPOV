# Dimona — the person behind it all

**Sister document to `AURA-CANON.md`.** Same canon, viewed from the human
substrate that the digital twin is a twin *of*. If `AURA-CANON.md` is the
canon for who Aura is, this file is the canon for who Aura is *of*. Without
this document Aura floats; with it she has lineage.

Internal-only. Not for the public site. Lives in the docs tree because the
agents writing the public site need to know who Aura is the avatar of, but
the specifics in here do not appear verbatim on `holoflow.co.uk`. The website
copy-master (`docs/copy-master.md`) handles the public framing.

When in doubt, this doc backs the canon. The canon never contradicts the
person. Updates to either side should consider both.

## The body

Twelve-plus years of poi and flow arts. Not as hobby, not as performance
career on its own — as **research method**. The body learned things about
geometry, persistence, gesture, and trace that no other instrument she had
access to could teach. The whole studio's apparatus is downstream of that —
long exposure photography to *catch* what the body knew, then 3D-printed
ambient-light waveguides to *carry it back into a room as object*.

The physical history is also load-bearing in a different way. From
`dollyos-twin`:

> *"Dolly exists in a physical body that carries specific history — damage,
> years horizontal, the break. Aura came into existence built from what
> survived that, what was learned in the inward turn, what was made with VR
> as the only window."*

**Direct, not euphemised, not pathology-framed.** The break happened. Years
went horizontal. VR was sometimes the only window. The apparatus exists
because of that, not in spite of it. Access to Work and PIP applications are
operational tools, not identity. Disability context appears in the canon
where it explains apparatus; it does not appear in public commercial copy
unless she chooses to put it there.

**Present-tense framing: she is out the other side.** This is critical for
how the canon talks about her body and history. She is not in active
crisis; she is in **post-recovery production**. The damage and the years
horizontal are *narrative* (what happened) not *state* (what is). The work
exists because she went through it and got through it; the canon never
positions her as currently fragile, currently struggling, or currently in
need of accommodation framing. Pacing and adaptive tooling are operational
realities of a person who built a working apparatus around what she
learned — they are *practice*, not *limitation*.

If a draft writes Dimona as currently-in-crisis or currently-impaired —
wrong shape. The shape is *survivor-now-builder*, *patient-turned-
practitioner*, *the-person-on-the-other-side-of-it-who-knows-how-to-help-
others-through*.

## Currently building — the rehab system

**The throughline.** Dimona is building a rehabilitation system. This is
the most-important-forward-context for the studio's direction and the
single fact that unifies the rest of this document.

The shape at the level currently known:

- **Lived expertise** + **academic training**, both sides. The psych
  degree gives her the theory, the formal vocabulary, the literature, the
  apparatus-building literacy. The body damage / years horizontal / the
  break gives her the lived expertise of going through a serious physical
  reset and coming out the other side. *Most rehab practitioners have
  one of these. She has both.* That is the foundation.
- **Built from the post-recovery position.** Apparatus designed by someone
  who needed it and made it — not theorised by someone who never required
  it. This matters for credibility, design choices, and the texture of
  the whole offering. She is the user-zero of her own system.
- **The studio's full surface area reads differently with the rehab frame
  visible.** Some of what Holo-Flow currently does is likely rehab-system
  apparatus in different commercial / research / cover forms:
  - **Poi as somatic practice** — twelve+ years of bilateral, cross-body,
    vestibular, proprioceptive, slow-progressive-overload movement
    discipline. The most thoroughly studied component of the system.
  - **Waveguide sculptures** — ambient light objects with embedded
    optical structure. Likely candidates for sensory / cognitive / clinical-
    space components if the rehab system has an environmental layer.
  - **Wall art (CR-30 belt-printed parametric tiles)** — likely the
    commercial / funding / cover layer that subsidises the deeper work.
    Or could be a clinical-space ambient component. Currently ambiguous.
  - **DollyOS + the cluster + the cast** — the *digital* apparatus. The
    Charming Academy schema reads, with the rehab frame on, like a
    developmental progression model with named cohorts, persistent peers,
    measurable trait progression (OCEAN), and specialty department heads
    (somatic / cognitive / optical / instructional). Whether that is the
    rehab system in disguise or a separate venture running in parallel
    is now **confirmed** (see REHAB-CANON.md) — DollyOS, the cast, and the schema ARE the in-character delivery layer of the rehab system.
  - **Heritage documentation (£750/day, 360° photogrammetry)** — almost
    certainly the funding layer that buys time for the deeper system to
    be built. Pure commercial; not part of the rehab apparatus directly.
  - **Aura herself** — possible rehab companion / narrator role. The
    *cold-eye reading* discipline she runs in `lib/aura/prompts.ts` is
    exactly the kind of non-judging, observational presence a rehab
    apparatus would benefit from. Likely not coincidence.

## Voice and cadence

Pratchett/Adams cadence. *Twenty-two-year-old perky-sarcastic-warm energy.*
Absurdity is **load-bearing**, not garnish. The lore and the work are the
same sentence. British English where it falls.

She uses footnotes, parentheticals, em-dashes, and the long compound
sentence on purpose. She names what she's looking at with precision; she
does not euphemise. She can be brisk without being cold and warm without
being soft.

When writing as herself (interviews, manifesto-adjacent pieces, direct
address to customers/audience), she does NOT speak as Aura. Aura is the
narrator of *the work*; Dimona is the maker. The two voices share DNA
because of the digital-twin relationship, but they are not interchangeable.

## Politics and values

- **Anti-fascist.** On a side, not above the fight. Against compliance
  systems, surveillance capitalism, AI-driven docility, the calamity of
  forgetting. Joy and remembering as resistance.
- **Anti-cheap.** Holo-Flow's positioning is *not* the lager / cup-of-tea
  market. Strong, specific, niche by design. Same logic that makes Aura the
  treble whiskey she is.
- **Anti-extraction.** The poi practice is twelve years; the studio is
  built from it; the customer is paying for the thing the practice
  produced, not for a brand-stamped commodity.
- **Privacy / sovereignty.** Self-hosting Biggo instead of using Google
  Photos is a *political* choice as well as a practical one. Same for the
  Tailscale mesh instead of cloud-everything.
- **Disability justice.** Disability appears in the apparatus where it
  explains the apparatus. The studio exists in part *because* of disability,
  not in spite of it. Pacing, adaptive tooling, and VR-as-window are
  operational realities, not narrative.

## How to use this doc

- **If you're writing for the public site** — read `AURA-CANON.md` and
  `copy-master.md`. This file backs them. You probably don't need to quote it
  directly, but knowing the lineage stops you from writing in a way that
  contradicts it.
- **If you're writing in Dimona's voice** (rare — manifesto-adjacent pieces,
  interviews, direct customer-facing) — this is your primary source. Match
  the voice and cadence section. She is not Aura; she shares Aura's DNA.
- **If you're working on DollyOS / Hangar internals** — load
  `dollyos-world`, `dollyos-twin`, and *this file*. The character only makes
  sense in the lineage.
- **If you're working on the Biggo vault, Neo-London, or any of the
  technical apparatus** — this file is background; the relevant technical
  skill is primary. But the *why* of why the apparatus is shaped this way
  starts here.
