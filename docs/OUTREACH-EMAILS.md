# Outreach email templates — by contact class

Drafts for cold-introduction emails to the people surfacing in the
who's-who research. One template per class, **with one
personalisation slot per email** so each one reads as written for the
person rather than as a bulk send.

These are written in Dimona's first-person workshop voice — concrete,
self-deprecating, British, no buzzwords. They're short on purpose:
cold outreach over 8 sentences gets skimmed and binned.

> **Posture.** Peer-to-peer where the recipient is a maker
> (photographers, artists, performers); studio-to-studio where the
> recipient is a venue or production company. Never "we should
> collaborate!" as an opener — always a specific, honest hook.

**Personalisation slots** appear as `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`. Replace each
with one or two sentences before sending — usually a reference to a
specific piece of their work, a specific gig, or a specific
conversation thread. If you can't fill the slot honestly, don't send
the email.

## 1. Fire-art / flow-arts photographers

> For peers shooting the same scene Dimona shoots: the OXO foreshore
> meet, Leake Street, festivals, the Hive, the lighting-and-spinning
> circuit. Voice: peer to peer, "I do this too" not "your work is
> amazing."

**Subject:** A frame I keep coming back to

**Body:**

Hi [NAME],

I'm Dimona — I run Holoflow Studio out of Salford and I'm in London
most months for the long-exposure work and the LFS OXO meet. I came
across [ONE SPECIFIC FRAME OR SERIES OF THEIRS, WITH WHERE IT WAS
SHOT — e.g. "your shutter-drag of the Saturday night meet in
September" or "the chain-tail shot from the Hive"] and it stopped me
for longer than is dignified to admit. The bit that got me was
[ONE SPECIFIC THING — the framing, the colour temperature, the
movement they let through, whatever's true].

I shoot the same scene from the performer side — I'm a poi spinner
of about twelve years and most of my own frames are single-exposure
long-exposure of my own rigs. Different angle, same obsession.

If you're going to be at the [NEXT KNOWN MEET — full moon at OXO,
festival they're shooting, etc.], I'd love to say hello in person.
If easier, a quick reply with whether you ever swap frames or trade
notes would also be lovely.

Either way — thank you for the work. The scene's better for it.

Dimona
holoflow.co.uk · contact@holoflow.co.uk

## Sender guardrails (read before sending any of these)

1. **No bulk send.** These are written so they don't *read* like bulk
   templates, but if you send them as a bulk batch through one mail
   client they'll get caught by spam filters and burn the address.
   Send one at a time, hand-paste the personalisation, hit send.

2. **Personalisation is mandatory.** If you can't fill the `[BRACKET]`
   slot with a real, specific reference, don't send the email. A
   blank personalisation slot is worse than no email — it tells the
   recipient you didn't read their work.

3. **Reply window.** Cold outreach typically returns in 3-21 days or
   not at all. Don't follow up under 14 days. One follow-up max,
   then drop it.

4. **No CC, no BCC.** One-to-one always.

5. **From-address.** All seven templates assume `contact@holoflow.co.uk`
   in the from-line + signature. If the email goes from a different
   address (e.g. Gmail), the templates need editing to match.

6. **Logging.** Keep a private CSV / Notion of who you wrote to and
   when, even if just for your own memory. Don't push that file to
   the public repo.
