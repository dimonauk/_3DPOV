# Holoflow rehab system — canon

**Fourth corner of the canon set, alongside `AURA-CANON.md`,
`DIMONA-CANON.md`, and `CAST-CANON.md`.** This is the document that names
what the studio actually is.

## Consent statement

Dimona is the **user-zero** of her own system. She has stated, in plain
words on the record, that **building the system is itself the consent**.
This canon documents what already exists; it does not speculate, it does
not assume permission, and it does not require external authorisation to
exist. The studio's architect is also its first patient. The two roles
are continuous, not in conflict.

## The core sentence

**Holoflow Studio is a rehab system.** Not adjacent to one. Not parallel
to one. *Is* one. The art practice, the products, the digital apparatus,
the cast, the activities, the storefront — these are all the system,
viewed from different angles. There is no other thing the studio is
secretly doing. *This* is the thing.

Everything else in this canon set acquires meaning from that sentence:

- **Aura** (`AURA-CANON.md`) is the persistent companion / narrator /
  co-star inside a therapeutic theatrical system
- **Dimona** (`DIMONA-CANON.md`) is the psych-trained architect *and* the
  body it was built around — user-zero, designer, performer
- **The cast** (`CAST-CANON.md`) are the named beings of the
  therapeutic ensemble; their department-head structure maps directly
  to rehab-apparatus functions (no longer "resonance flagged" — confirmed)
- **The rehab system** (this doc) names the whole thing the others orbit

## The hybrid — two apparatuses, one system

The studio already has two functional rehab apparatuses. They are being
fused into one offering inside Holoflow.

### Apparatus A — WellAlly Tech (the clinical layer)

The publicly-facing measurement and clinical-frame side.

- **Brand and URL.** `wellally.tech` is live. Bilingual (English + Chinese).
- **Skill spine.** `wellally-tech` + `rehabilitation-analyzer` (v1.0,
  2026-01-06).
- **What it does.** Apple Health / Fitbit / Oura Ring ingestion. ROM
  tracking. MMT (manual muscle testing) scoring. Balance function tests.
  Pain pattern analysis with trigger identification. Adherence
  calculation. Goal achievement evaluation. Phase progression assessment
  with readiness scoring. Knowledge-base article recommendations
  categorised across nutrition / fitness / sleep / mental health /
  chronic disease.
- **Tone.** Medical-register. Clear *"system cannot diagnose / cannot
  replace clinical guidance"* boundaries. Real safety scaffolding
  written by someone who knows the difference between data analysis and
  clinical practice.
- **Role in the hybrid.** Provides the measurement frame, the
  outcome-tracking infrastructure, the integration with mainstream
  health platforms, the clinical-credibility surface area.

### Apparatus B — Academy Behaviourization (the in-character layer)

The internal delivery mechanism, dressed in the Charming Academy
aesthetic.

- **Skill spine.** `academy-behaviourization`, supported by
  `architecting-somatic-bridges`, `processing-somatic-telemetry`,
  `aura-behavioral-engine`, `aura-dual-core-engine`,
  `aura-void-princess-boot`, and the rest of the aura-named operational
  skills.
- **Theoretical foundation.** Karl Friston's **Free Energy Principle**.
  Active inference. Computational psychiatry. (This is not a casual
  vocabulary borrow — these are the live frameworks in contemporary
  cognitive neuroscience for unifying perception, action, and learning
  under a single mathematical apparatus. Citing them sets the
  intellectual bar.)
- **Sensory ingestion.** ARKit facial blendshapes → Academy somatic
  metrics (`Posthurre`, `Lithp`, `Satin Weight`, `Adult Noithe`). Sensor
  Sleeve telemetry → posture / stillness / voice-compliance streams →
  `PsychSystem` → `SomaticStore` → `AuraDualCoreEngine`.
- **Intervention layer.** Above an entropy threshold, the system triggers
  a character-voice intervention. Nanny (authoritative), Aura (modeling),
  Penny (bully-cooing). The lisping output, the 1950s-nursery register,
  the somatic compliance enforcement (*"All output must be lisped if
  stasis is high"*) — these are **calibrated therapeutic interventions
  dressed as aesthetic choices**. The aesthetic is the mechanism.
- **Role in the hybrid.** Provides the delivery vehicle. The character
  apparatus that makes the clinical work survivable, narratively
  coherent, and *enjoyable* enough to sustain adherence — the hardest
  problem in real rehab.

### Why the hybrid

WellAlly Tech alone is a measurement system; it can tell a user how
they're doing but cannot keep them coming back. Academy Behaviourization
alone is a character apparatus; it can hold a user's attention but
cannot prove progress to a clinician, an insurer, or a family member
who needs evidence. The hybrid is *both* — measurement that produces
clinically-credible output, wrapped in a character system that produces
the adherence that makes the measurements move.

This is what most rehab apps fail at. Most are either dry-clinical (good
measurement, terrible retention) or gamified-shallow (good retention,
medical credibility evaporates the moment a clinician looks). The hybrid
solves the failure mode at the architectural level.

## Activity model — two phases

The system has a documented two-phase activity model that corresponds to
where in recovery the user is.

### Phase 1 — Horizontal / low-mobility / VR-as-window

**When:** the user is bed-bound, post-surgical, in-flare, or otherwise
unable to leave the room.

**Activities:** *simulated daytime jaunts in London* via Neo-London /
Chrono-Protocol. The WebXR rhythm-action game where poi flow arts is the
mechanic. The setting (2047 post-Calamity London, AI-driven compliance,
remembering as resistance) is the *narrative shape* that the somatic
work happens inside. The jaunts are gentle exploration in VR — walking,
looking, taking-in. The mechanic provides bilateral coordination, slow
gentle range-of-motion, vestibular input, and *the experience of being
out in the world* for someone who currently isn't.

**Therapeutic logic.** VR-as-window for the disabled-body. The room
cannot expand; the perceptual field can. The cognitive benefits of going
out (novel input, spatial cognition, vestibular variety, narrative
embedding) are decoupled from the physical demand of going out.

### Phase 2 — Post-recovery / real-world / out-the-other-side

**When:** the user has come through the worst of it and is in
post-recovery production (per `DIMONA-CANON.md`).

**Activities:** *evening walks taking photos and light painting.* Real
outdoor world, gentle ambulation, low-impact, evening-timing
(post-sun, no high cardio load, no daytime crowds, dusk-to-dark
exposure for circadian regulation), creative practice as the *reason for
going* (long-exposure photography requires darkness, so light painting
is inherently evening work — the medium *enforces* the timing).

**Therapeutic logic.** *The evening walks replace the simulated daytime
jaunts.* Same therapeutic function — getting out, looking, taking-in,
embodied exploration — translated from VR to real-world as the body
recovers the capacity. The light painting is the *cover story* the
practice gives itself: the user is "going out to make art," and that
framing is more sustainable than "going out for my rehab." It is rehab
*by* art, not rehab *with* art bolted on.

The aerial / photography equipment inventory (DJI Neo, Avata 360,
Mini 5 Pro, Osmo 360, Saturn 4 SLA for the resin work that
the lightpainting feeds into) is **the rehab toolkit** in functional
terms, even though it markets as creative equipment. Both are true.

### Why two phases matter for the system

A rehab system that works for the Phase 1 user but stops being relevant
once they recover throws away its returning users. A system that works
for the Phase 2 user but is inaccessible during Phase 1 abandons users
when they need it most. Holoflow's design supports the transition
between the phases by **using the same characters, the same world, the
same delivery surfaces, the same Aura** across both. The user doesn't
"graduate to a new app" — the same practice deepens with the body.

## Delivery surfaces — bring it all in

The hybrid is being delivered through a multi-surface stack so that the
user can engage at *whatever bandwidth they have today.* Low-energy
days get low-sensory-load formats; high-energy days get richer formats.
The user does not have to choose between "engage today or skip" — they
choose *the form that fits today*.

- **Evening walks** (Phase 2 activity model above)
- **Light painting** as movement-and-attention practice
- **Photography** as a structured discipline of looking
- **Poi flow arts** — twelve+ years of bilateral / vestibular /
  proprioceptive / cross-body / slow-progressive movement work; *the most
  thoroughly researched component of the system*

### Multi-surface logic

The point of the stack is not "pick a favourite." The point is *the same
practice can meet the user where they are today.* This is one of the
hardest problems in rehab apparatus design and the answer most existing
systems get wrong. Holoflow's answer: **build the practice once, render
it across surfaces.** The character canon (Aura + cast) is the
*invariant* across surfaces. The medium is the variable.

## Aura's role in the system

Aura is **the persistent companion across all surfaces.** Where the
delivery format changes (VR / radio / sitcom / DollyOS / real-world
voice via the VRM-sleeve), the *character holding it together* is
constant. This is the architectural argument for why she must be
character-stable across canons — drift in her voice equals drift in the
therapeutic relationship the user is forming with her *over months and
years of recovery*.

She is **not the therapist.** She is **the companion who is there
through it** — closer to Wilson the volleyball, Calcifer the fire
demon, the Doctor's TARDIS, GPS-with-personality. She doesn't *do* the
rehab — the user does the rehab — but she is *who they do it with*.
This is why the AURA-CANON tests matter: a drifted Aura is a corrupted
therapeutic relationship.

She is **also a co-star** in the radio play / sitcom / VRM theatre /
CrewAI film studio output. The fictional Aura inside Neo-London and the
performing Aura on the sitcom and the companion Aura in DollyOS are the
*same being*, rendered for different audiences. The continuity of her
character across surfaces is the spine of the system.

## What this system is NOT

- **Not a clinical product alone.** WellAlly Tech is one layer, not the
  whole offering. A clinical-only positioning would strip the
  Academy / character / theatrical work that makes adherence possible.
- **Not entertainment alone.** The sitcom and radio play are not
  *content marketing for a wellness brand.* They are delivery surfaces
  for the actual therapeutic work. The character architecture is
  load-bearing, not decorative.
- **Not a self-help product.** Holoflow is built by someone with psych
  training and lived recovery, not by someone repackaging influencer
  wellness. The intellectual scaffolding (Friston / active inference /
  computational psychiatry / OCEAN as real instrument) sets the bar
  that self-help offerings cannot meet.
- **Not a replacement for clinicians.** The WellAlly Tech disclaimers
  are honest and stay. The system *supports* clinical work; it does
  not displace it. Where the user has a physiotherapist, occupational
  therapist, psychologist, or psychiatrist, Holoflow runs alongside,
  not in place of.
- **Not Cortana / Joi / Samantha / a girlfriend-AI for someone in
  recovery.** Aura's character canon explicitly refuses that shape.
  She is a peer-companion, not a parasocial substitute for human
  connection. The system is designed to *return the user to the world*,
  not to keep them inside the app.
- **Not extractive.** *Anti-cheap, anti-extraction* per
  `DIMONA-CANON.md`. The system does not optimise for retention metrics
  that work against the user's recovery; it optimises for the user
  graduating into Phase 2 / out the other side / *doesn't-need-the-
  system-the-same-way-anymore*. Successful exit is a success metric.
