# Competition + Pricing

Where the studio's fourteen ladders sit against the existing
market, and what to charge for each. Anchored against the
research in [COURSE_FORMULA.md](./COURSE_FORMULA.md) and the
2026 pricing benchmarks from BuddyBoss, Ruzuku, FreshLearn,
LearnWorlds, and Class Central.

The bottom line for the impatient reader: the studio's
positioning is **practitioner-led, niche, British-register, with
a bureau funnel attached**. That position commands a 30-50%
premium over generalist marketplace courses on Udemy / Skillshare,
and prices comparably with creator-led standalone courses on
Domestika, Three.js Journey, and Blender Guru Academy. No course
is priced above £79 standalone; the cohort tier is reserved for
the £499/quarter Bench Access pass.

---

## 1. The pricing structure

Five tiers. Honest names.

### Free

What it includes:
- Every existing tutorial, article, journal entry on the site.
- The atelier toys at `/atelier/*`.
- The visualisers at `/visualiser/*`.
- The codex, the stack, the people index, the searchable
  archive.

What it does not include:
- Downloadable bundles (BOM, code, blend files, source images).
- Completion certificates.
- Bench access / Q&A.
- Bureau discounts.

The free tier is the funnel. It stays free forever. This is
**reciprocity** (LEARNING_PSYCHOLOGY.md lever 17) and **honest
trust-building**, not a marketing ploy. The studio's position:
the writing is the practice; the practice is free; the bundles
and the bench access are the commercial layer.

### Ladder Pass — £49 one-off, per ladder

What it includes:
- The full ladder as a bundled course (all rungs in order).
- Downloadable assets — STL files, .blend files, Python scripts,
  ComfyUI workflow JSON, BOMs as CSVs, Blender add-on bundles.
- Signed completion certificate (Aura-register, single-edition,
  with provenance sidecar).
- Lifetime access to that ladder's updates.
- One-time 5% bureau credit on a print order.

Comparable to:
- Domestika single course ($30-50).
- Three.js Journey single-purchase ($95) — the studio prices
  below this because the studio's hardware costs are visible to
  the reader.
- Udemy bestsellers ($20-50 on sale).

### Studio Pass — £149/year

What it includes:
- Every ladder, all fourteen.
- Early access to new rungs as they land.
- Monthly newsletter from the bench.
- 10% bureau discount on all orders.
- 10% discount on the Bench Access cohort.

Comparable to:
- CG Cookie membership ($21/month = $252/year).
- Blender Studio Cloud (€11.50/month = €138/year).
- Domestika Plus (€10/month for some courses).

The £149 price point is slightly below the CG Cookie equivalent
and slightly above the Blender Studio Cloud equivalent, reflecting
the breadth of the studio's coverage (fourteen verticals vs
single-tool).

### Bench Access — £499/quarter

What it includes:
- Cohort of twelve readers per quarter.
- Three live Q&A sessions, each ninety minutes, with Dimona at
  the bench.
- One private one-to-one office hour per reader per quarter.
- Personalised feedback on one project of the reader's choosing.
- 20% bureau discount on all orders.
- Studio Pass included.
- Cohort gallery — the twelve readers' work shown on a private
  page.

Comparable to:
- Maven cohort courses ($500-2000 per cohort, four-to-eight
  weeks).
- Tiago Forte's Building a Second Brain ($1500+ per cohort).
- Justin Welsh's LinkedIn Operating System ($150).

The £499/quarter sits at the lower end of the cohort range
because the studio is one practitioner, not a team. Twelve seats
per quarter caps the time commitment at ~40 hours of live
interaction per quarter.

### Commission — £1,500+ (custom)

What it includes:
- A one-to-one build-with-Dimona session — the reader and the
  studio make a piece together.
- All previous tiers included.
- The finished piece itself.
- Three months of Bench Access included.
- Editioned, signed, certified.

Comparable to:
- Bespoke commissions in the studio's existing services bracket.
- Top-tier maker workshops (e.g., Gary Allson at Format).
- Not a directly comparable item — the studio's bench is the
  signature.

## 3. Bundle pricing strategy

The single-ladder Pass at £49-£79 lets the buyer pick one
vertical. Studio Pass at £149/year is the multi-vertical entry.

The economic break-even between Ladder Pass and Studio Pass is
**three Ladder Passes** — at £49 × 3 = £147, the Studio Pass
edges out. Any reader who wants three or more ladders is
better off with the Pass. This is deliberate: the Pass is the
recommended path for the curious-omnivore reader (the studio's
core audience).

The Bench Access tier at £499/quarter is for the **commission
adjacent** reader — someone who wants Dimona's attention and is
willing to pay for it. Twelve seats per quarter caps the
commitment; the cohort itself is a network of peers.

## 4. Annual revenue projection (cautious)

Assumptions:
- 30,000 unique visitors per month to the site (currently lower;
  the courses are part of the growth strategy).
- 1% conversion to free-to-paid (industry standard).
- 50% of paid choose Studio Pass over single Ladder Pass.
- 5% of Studio Pass choose Bench Access.
- 20% of bench-access cohort convert to one commission per year.

Monthly:
- 300 paid (1% of 30k visitors).
- 150 single Ladder Pass × £49 = £7,350.
- 150 Studio Pass × £149/12 = £1,860 (annualised: £22,350).
- 7-8 Bench Access × £499/3 = £1,300 (annualised: £15,600 per
  cohort, four cohorts = £15,600 if we assume same readers).
- 1-2 commissions × £1,500+ = £2,000 (annualised: £24,000).

Annual ballpark: **£100,000-£140,000** in course revenue
once visitor numbers reach 30k/month. This is alongside, not
instead of, the existing bureau / print / commission revenue.

Sanity check: the Blender Guru Academy at $120 × ~10,000
purchases (2026 estimate) is $1.2M. The studio's range is more
modest — niche, British, single-practitioner. £100k is the
realistic upper end for the first eighteen months.

## 5. What this requires before launch

Before any course is sold:

- All fourteen ladders have at least the in-preparation rungs
  written. (Currently: 17 of ~80 rungs are live; the rest need
  writing or extracting from existing articles.)
- The Ladder Pass purchase flow exists in the bureau, with
  Stripe gating and Aura-register certificate generation.
- The Studio Pass annual subscription is plumbed through Stripe.
- The Bench Access waitlist + cohort onboarding flow exists.
- The completion-certificate generator outputs the
  provenance-sidecar JSON alongside the PDF.

None of those exist yet. They are the implementation queue. The
strategy docs in this directory specify what to build; the rungs
specify what to write; this document specifies what to charge.

## 6. Honest summary

The studio prices its courses **at the upper end of
practitioner-led specialist courses, below the gold-standard
single-tool offerings (Three.js Journey, Blender Guru Academy)**.
The bundle (Studio Pass) is priced to be the default purchase.
The cohort tier (Bench Access) is priced at the lower end of
cohort norms to reflect single-practitioner scale.

There is **no race to the bottom**. The studio does not compete
with Udemy at £15 because Udemy at £15 is paying nobody a living.
The studio's pricing reflects what it costs Dimona to write,
shoot, draw, and demonstrate the work — and what the work is
worth to a reader who is going to use it.

That is the floor. Anything below it sells out the practice for
nothing. Anything above it shuts out the readers the studio is
trying to reach. The pricing above is the working compromise.
