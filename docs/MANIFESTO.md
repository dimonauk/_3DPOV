# Manifesto — what the bench is for

This document names the studio's commitments to the people who
encounter the work. The article `articles/art-as-door-five-layers`
is the public-voice variant of Section II; this file is the
canonical record of the five-layer architecture and the first
principles underneath it.

The bench's claim is small and specific: **the work speaks first.**
No biography is required to encounter the object. Everything else
follows from that one rule.

## I. Origin — fifteen years of gravity

The project did not begin with a technology stack. It began with a
body moving through space, fifteen years ago, and a question:
**what does movement leave behind?**

Poi practice is circular, flowing, held in both hands, tracing arcs
that exist for a fraction of a second before vanishing. The writing
disappears. The body remembers. The audience carries an impression
they cannot reproduce or fully articulate.

For fifteen years the question was: can the geometry be made
permanent? Not as a video. Not as a long exposure. As an **object**
— a thing that contains the movement, not a representation of it.

The answer required a convergence that did not exist until now:

- Movement capture precise enough to record position, velocity and
  acceleration of each hand at every moment.
- 3D printing at resolution sufficient to form functional optical
  structures (SLA at ~19 µm XY).
- Physics understanding deep enough to design waveguides, gratings,
  and biomimetic structures that make the object interact with
  light in movement-specific ways.
- AI collaboration capable of holding the full knowledge space —
  optics, biology, choreography, computation — simultaneously, in
  service of a specific human vision.

None of these existed together before approximately 2024. The
fifteen years were not wasted — they were preparation. The
convergence is now. The arc is documented in
`articles/the-convergence`.

## II. The five-layer architecture — art as door

The work is built as a deliberate architecture of layers. Each
layer stands alone; each invites the next for those who want it.

| Layer | What it is | For whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — The object | Beautiful, luminous, interactive with light. Requires no knowledge of the creator. | Anyone. Galleries. Collectors. Press. |
| 2 — The physics | The waveguides. The structural colour. The biomimicry. The diffraction. The mathematics. | Engineers, scientists, makers, curious minds. |
| 3 — The movement | The poi data. The capture session. The specific dance, the specific moment. | Dancers, performers, movement artists. |
| 4 — The system | The evolutionary engine. The generational breeding. The Laban encoding. The AI collaboration. | Technologists, computational artists, systems thinkers. |
| 5 — The person | A disabled trans woman who spent fifteen years building toward a convergence that only became possible now. | Those who pull all the threads. |

Layer 1 stands completely alone. It is what galleries show,
collectors buy, press photographs. The rabbit hole is there for
those who want to pull the threads.

The article `articles/art-as-door-five-layers` is the public-voice
account of this architecture. The five-layer table above is the
canonical record.

### The self-selecting door

The architecture creates a natural filter without any active
gatekeeping:

- People who approach with curiosity find ever-deepening layers.
  The work rewards them.
- People who approach with cruelty get a pretty object they don't
  understand. The door doesn't open. They have gated themselves
  out.

This is not manipulation. It is generosity. Everyone gets something
beautiful. Those who bring openness get everything.

The cruel are not fought. They are architecturally irrelevant.

## III. Provenance as art

Most generative art produces **universal** objects. A Mandelbulb is
the same Mandelbulb regardless of who computed it. The mathematics
is beautiful; the provenance is anonymous.

This studio produces **specific** objects. Each sculpture is
causally entangled with:

- A specific body (the artist's body — arm length, habitual
  extension radius, timing).
- A specific movement practice (the artist's poi patterns — the way
  she initiates a butterfly versus a weave).
- A specific moment (this session, this song, this day).

The sculpture cannot be reproduced without the source data. You
cannot reverse-engineer the movement from the object — but you
cannot fabricate the object without the movement either. The
provenance is physical. It is encoded in the geometry, the grating
pitch, the waveguide topology. It is not metadata. It is the
object itself.

The article `articles/provenance-as-discipline` is the operational
account: every fabrication leaves the bench with a JSON certificate
naming its parentage chain, kingdom, channel, fitness score,
oracle gate result and narrative. Not metadata. Material identity.

## IV. The five convergences

The bench draws from five biological systems that solved the same
problem nature solved — distributing, concentrating, and spectrally
separating light. Each is a real physical mechanism, not a
decorative reference.

| Typology | Borrowed from | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gyroid | *Callophrys rubi* butterfly wing scales | Dual-channel labyrinth; structural colour through channel-crossing. |
| Turing reaction–diffusion | Seashell pigmentation, leopard spots | Velocity field seeds the pattern; texture modulates waveguide leakage. |
| Whispering gallery mode | St Paul's Cathedral; microsphere resonators | Toroidal trapping by continuous TIR; resonance at designed escape features. |
| Ctenophore chirped grating | Comb jellies | Velocity-mapped groove pitch; movement-encoded spectral distribution. |
| Physarum network | *Physarum polycephalum* slime mould | Optimal waveguide topology emerged through simulation; Murray's Law tube radii. |

The physics behind each is documented in `PHYSICS_AND_OPTICS.md`.
The breeding loop that selects between them is documented in
`articles/how-the-studio-breeds-sculptures`. The taxonomy of the
kingdoms underneath the five typologies is in
`articles/the-eight-kingdoms`.

## V. First principles — non-negotiable

These do not move. Every other decision is downstream of them.

1. **The work speaks first.** No biography required to encounter or
   appreciate the objects.
2. **The physics is real.** Not decoration, not metaphor. The
   waveguide works. The diffraction diffracts. The grating produces
   an actual spectrum.
3. **The movement is irreplaceable.** Each object is causally
   entangled with a specific body's specific movement at a specific
   moment. This provenance is the artistic value.
4. **The evolution is genuine.** Sculptures are bred, not designed.
   The aesthetic emerges through selection pressure over
   generations.
5. **The system is offline-first.** No cloud dependency. No
   subscription. No data leaves the machine. Creative sovereignty
   is absolute.
6. **The threads are generous.** The layers of meaning are there for
   anyone curious enough to pull. Nobody is excluded except those
   who choose exclusion.
7. **The cruel are architecturally irrelevant.** The work does not
   fight prejudice. It makes prejudice unable to reach the centre.

## VI. The commitment to the user

Anyone encountering the work has a contract with the bench:

- The object is complete. You are not buying an explanation.
- If you ask how it works, the physics is true and the answer is
  not metaphorical.
- If you ask what it contains, the movement data is there and
  reproducible from the JSON.
- If you ask how it was made, the system is documented — this
  file, `PHYSICS_AND_OPTICS.md`, `METHODOLOGY.md`,
  `articles/the-convergence`, `articles/how-the-studio-breeds-sculptures`.
- If you ask who made it, the person is at the centre of the
  labyrinth. Not hidden. Not announced. Reachable for those who
  walk the threads.

The work owes its viewer all five layers. It does not owe a
viewer five layers' worth of attention. The architecture is built
so that one layer is enough.
